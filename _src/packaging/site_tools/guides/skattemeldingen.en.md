Crypto goes in the tax return (skattemeldingen) under **Finans → Virtuelle eiendeler / kryptovaluta**. You can use one combined card for all your coins.

## The fields
Kryptokompis shows the figures under **Tax report → For the tax return** and in chapter 1 of the PDF report:

| Field in the tax return | What it is |
|---|---|
| Formuesverdi (wealth) | The value of your coins on 31.12 |
| Skattepliktig gevinst (taxable gain) | All sales and swaps with a gain |
| Fradragsberettiget tap (deductible loss) | All sales and swaps with a loss |
| Andre inntekter (other income) | Staking, rewards and other income in coins |
| Forvaltningskostnader (management costs) | Costs you entered yourself, e.g. software |

## Gains and losses separately
Gains and losses go in separate fields. Do not net them against each other before entering them.

## A swap is a sale too
Swapping one coin for another - including for a stablecoin such as USDT or USDC - is a realisation. The gain or loss is calculated in NOK on the day of the swap. Transfers between your own accounts are not sales.

## Cash on exchanges
Dollars and kroner held on an exchange on 31.12 are wealth, but not a virtual asset. Kryptokompis shows the balance separately so you can report it as a deposit or claim abroad.

## Keep the documentation
The PDF and CSV files are not submitted, but they are your documentation if the tax administration asks.

*This is not tax advice. Read more at [Skatteetaten](https://www.skatteetaten.no/en/person/taxes/get-the-taxes-right/virtual-currency/), and ask an accountant if unsure.*
