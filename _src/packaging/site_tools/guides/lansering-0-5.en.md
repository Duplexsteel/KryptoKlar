Kryptokompis 0.5 is out for Windows and Android.

## New name
The app used to be called KryptoKlar. If you update from an older version, Kryptokompis moves your data, keys and backups over by itself. Nothing to do.

## The portfolio
- Every coin from every exchange added up, valued in NOK.
- Change over the last 24 hours, in NOK and percent, and each coin's share.
- Refreshes itself every two minutes while the app is open.
- Average cost and unrealised gain or loss per coin.

The portfolio is free. The figures for the tax return, the PDF report and the CSV files need a licence.

## Also
- A new look, matching the website.
- Management costs can be entered in the app and are included in the report.
