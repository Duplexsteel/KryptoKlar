How it works

Four steps from exchange history to a finished tax return. Most of your work is connecting the exchanges the first time.

  1. Connect your exchanges

    With a read-only API key, or the exchange's own export file. Importing the same period again is safe - nothing is counted twice.

  2. KryptoKlar calculates

    FIFO per coin across all exchanges. Every purchase gets its NOK cost on its own date, at Norges Bank's rate.

  3. You review the checks

    Holdings are reconciled with what the exchanges say you own. If purchase history is missing, the app tells you instead of guessing.

  4. You enter the figures

    The figures are ready for the Virtuelle eiendeler card in your tax return. The PDF and CSV are your documentation if Skatteetaten asks.

How much do you have to do? Connect each exchange once, and add what the app cannot see - for example a wallet outside any exchange, or management costs such as software and a share of your internet.

Old losses can count again

Holding coins from the last bull run at a loss? Import the old exchange history and KryptoKlar calculates the real loss when you sell - never more than you actually lost.

How it is calculated

FIFO per coin
First purchase out first, across all your exchanges.
Cost in NOK
Fixed on the purchase date, at Norges Bank's daily rate.
Swapping coins
Is a sale, also to and from stablecoins.
Transfers between your own accounts
Are not a sale.
Fees paid in coin
Count as a small sale of that coin.
Staking and rewards
Income at market value, which also becomes the cost.
Gains and losses
Reported separately, never netted.
Wealth
Market value at 31.12. Cash on exchanges is shown separately.

The ruleset is called NO-krypto-2026.1 and is stated in every report.

Questions and answers

Is this tax advice?

No. KryptoKlar is a calculation tool that applies the Norwegian rules as it interprets them, and states the ruleset used in every report. You are responsible for your tax return - check the figures, and ask Skatteetaten or an accountant if unsure.

What about earlier years?

You choose where full FIFO starts. Years already filed another way stay closed. For coins where you have the full history, the real old purchase prices can be used - so a real loss can be claimed when you sell.

What about cash on exchanges?

Dollars and kroner held on an exchange are wealth, but not a virtual asset. KryptoKlar shows the 31.12 balance separately so you can report it as a deposit or claim abroad.

My exchange is not listed.

Use the import template for now, and tell us on Discord. New exchanges are added on request.

Which devices?

Windows PC and Android phone. One licence covers both. iPhone may come later.

How do I get help?

Ask on Discord, or send a message from the app: Data & setup, Feedback and requests.

Important: the result is only as complete as the data you give KryptoKlar. Transactions it never sees - other exchanges, wallets, history an exchange no longer provides - must be imported, or the result will be wrong. The checks help you find gaps.
