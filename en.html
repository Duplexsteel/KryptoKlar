What it does

A complete overview of your exchange history, calculated under the Norwegian rules and checked against what your exchanges show you hold - in one PDF you can use yourself or take to your accountant.

01 · COLLECT

All your exchanges

Read-only API sync, or the exchange's own statement file. Re-importing is safe: nothing is ever counted twice.

02 · CALCULATE

Norwegian rules, in NOK

FIFO per coin across all exchanges. Each purchase's NOK cost fixed on its own date. Coin-to-coin swaps, fees paid in coin, staking and futures included.

03 · CHECK

Nothing silently missing

Holdings are reconciled with your exchanges' real balances. Missing purchase history is flagged, never guessed.

04 · DOCUMENT

PDF + CSV record

The figures for the tax return, plus every sale with its FIFO working - the record you must be able to show if Skatteetaten asks.

05 · RECOVER

Old losses count

Coins from the last bull run sitting at a loss? Import the old history, sell, and deduct the real loss - never more than you actually lost.

06 · PRIVATE

Stays on your PC

No account, no cloud. KryptoKlar talks to your exchanges and to public price sources - nothing else.

FAQ

Is this tax advice?

No. KryptoKlar is a calculation tool that applies the Norwegian rules as it interprets them, and states which ruleset it used in every report. You are responsible for your tax return - check the figures, and ask Skatteetaten or an accountant when unsure.

Does my data leave my computer?

No. Your transactions, reports and settings stay on your PC. API keys are stored in Windows' credential store and used read-only. Beta feedback is sent only when you press Send, and contains no transaction data.

What about earlier years?

You choose where the full FIFO calculation starts. Years already filed another way stay closed. For coins where you have the complete history, the real old purchase prices can be used - so a real loss can be deducted when you sell.

My exchange isn't listed.

Use the import template for now, and tell us on Discord. New exchanges are added on request.

Which devices?

Windows PC and Android phone. One licence covers both.

Important: KryptoKlar's result is only as complete as the data you give it. Transactions it never sees - other exchanges, wallets, history an exchange no longer provides - must be imported, or the result will be wrong. The app shows checks to help you find gaps.
